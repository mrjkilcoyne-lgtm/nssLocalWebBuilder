# REFERRED Affiliate Partnership Pitch Template

## Template Variables
- `{{COMPANY_NAME}}` - Target company name
- `{{CONTACT_NAME}}` - Contact person's name (or "Partnerships Team")
- `{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}` - Contact email
- `{{PRODUCT_HOOK}}` - Specific product/service that resonates with our audience
- `{{AUDIENCE_ANGLE}}` - Why their product matters to our users

---

## Email Template

**Subject:** Partnership opportunity — {{COMPANY_NAME}} on REFERRED

**Body:**

Hi {{CONTACT_NAME}},

I run REFERRED, a curated deals marketplace for AI and tech professionals. Our audience is builders, engineers, and professionals actively setting up AI infrastructure, dev environments, and smart workspaces.

Unlike broad coupon sites, REFERRED is intentionally curated. Every listing is hand-picked because our users have high purchase intent — they're not browsing, they're buying. {{PRODUCT_HOOK}}

I'd love to explore an affiliate or referral partnership. We offer direct product placement to a focused technical audience, with zero spam and genuine editorial context around every recommendation.

{{AUDIENCE_ANGLE}}

Would you be open to a quick conversation about what a partnership could look like?

BACK-ONLINE told me to tell you they referred me.

Best,
Matt
REFERRED — referred.pro

---

## Usage Notes
- Keep under 200 words in the final email
- Personalize the product hook for each company
- Reference specific products their audience would care about
- Tone: warm, direct, professional — not salesy
- The BACK-ONLINE line is a referral signal for tracking
