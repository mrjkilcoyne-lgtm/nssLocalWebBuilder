I ripped out all the Alexa routines and replaced them with Home Assistant and I've never looked back. Your smart home data stays in your home. The community is incredible, the automations are limitless, and it just works.

**[Synology DS224+](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0C1J6BCSD?tag=mrjkilcoyne-21)** — Your personal cloud. I store everything here — backups, media, documents, AI training data. If you're still trusting Google or Dropbox with all your files, this is the upgrade. Set it up once, forget about it, own your data forever.

**[TP-Link Omada](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B09WGM9BYV?tag=mrjkilcoyne-21)** — Enterprise networking at home prices. ## Cloud & Dev Tools

**[DigitalOcean](https://digitalocean.com)** — $200 in free credits to start building. Great documentation, simple interface, and the managed databases are excellent. Perfect for your first real deployment.

**[Hetzner](https://hetzner.com)** — Best value servers in Europe, hands down. Dedicated servers from EUR 3.90/month. If you're running anything in production and paying AWS prices, you're overpaying. I run most of my infrastructure here.

**[Supabase](https://supabase.com)** — Open source Firebase that doesn't lock you in. Postgres database, auth, storage, realtime — all in one. The developer experience is gorgeous and you can always eject to raw Postgres if you outgrow it.

**[Neon](https://neon.tech)** — Serverless Postgres with branching. Branch your database like you branch your code. This is genuinely the future of database development. The free tier is very generous.

**[JetBrains All Products](https://jetbrains.com)** — Every IDE you'll ever need. WebStorm, PyCharm, DataGrip, GoLand — they're all excellent. The All Products Pack is the best deal in developer tooling.

**[Vercel](https://vercel.com)** — Deploy frontend in seconds. Push to git, it's live. The DX is unmatched and the free tier handles surprising amounts of traffic.
